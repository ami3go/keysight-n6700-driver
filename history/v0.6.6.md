# v0.6.6 (2026-10-10)

PR04 of [scope remediation](../docs/N679XA_SCOPE_REMEDIATION_PLAN.md). Added per-alias, per-thread acquisition lease protecting the capture buffer between configure and validated fetch/abort. All raw SCPI commands pass the lease guard, and interfering MEAS/RESET/FORMAT commands are rejected; rediscovery/session disconnect are disallowed while held. Emergency output OFF remains possible, and separate aliases remain independent. Added deterministic concurrency/negative tests.

**Limitations:** In-process per-driver lease only; no external multi-process/controller coordination. HIL qualification remains outstanding. PR05 handles SCPI validation, format and data integrity.
