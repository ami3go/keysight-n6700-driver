# v0.6.12 — 2026-10-10

Automated, noninteractive passive scope HIL runner for real N6700 mainframe + approved N6791A/N6792A module.

Includes Ethernet/VISA dual-gated approval, strict profile and module serial/mainframe IDN verification, unrelated-output OFF preflight without modification, voltage/current BUS trigger captures across ASCII/REAL32 and readback-confirmed timing, missing-trigger timeout and verified abort negative scenarios, per-run waveform SHA-256 and summary.json / junit.xml artifacts, fail-closed cleanup on errors and no hardware simulator fallback. Simulator-based orchestration unit tests run in CI; no physical bench connected.

[Usage and template](../docs/SCOPE_AUTOMATED_HIL.md). This release **does not qualify physical transient behavior** or waive independent DSO/repeated-trigger tests, external interlocks, reliability soak, and human sign-off. Production NO-GO remains until those requirements are met.
